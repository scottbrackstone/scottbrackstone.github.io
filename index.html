Turn the assignment structure into usable interface sections.

04 Mar 2026

Object-oriented programming started to feel more natural

What I learned
OOP takes repeated practice before it starts to feel natural.
What was difficult

Next step

About this site

A quiet place for progress notes

The focus is on the work and the learning process, not on making a big personal brand out of it.

Why this exists

I am studying IT and using this site to document projects, coursework, experiments, and the small lessons that come from building things. Some notes will be rough because they are written while I am still learning.

The aim is to keep a useful record: what I tried, what worked, what did not, and where the next improvement might be. If a project becomes more complete, it can earn its own page later.

  • Keep the tone honest and practical.
  • Show the learning process, not just finished output.
  • Prefer useful notes over polished hype.
  • Keep the site easy to maintain between assignments.
