Echo Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Who this covers

Echo is a personal assistant that one person built for their own use. It runs on that person's own computer. The only Google account Echo connects to is its owner's. Echo is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data Echo accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): the sender, subject, date and short preview of messages, and a message's content only when the owner asks for that message.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): event titles, times and places.

Echo cannot send, delete, label or change email, and cannot create or change calendar events.

How it is used

  • Only to answer the owner's own requests (for example, "find my flight booking") and, if the owner switches them on, for the owner's daily briefing and reminders.
  • To produce an answer, the relevant email or calendar details are sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen (currently Anthropic's Claude API). They are processed under that provider's commercial API terms. Echo does not use this data to train AI models.
  • Google data is never sold, shared with other people, used for advertising, or read by anyone other than the owner.

Storage and security

Echo stores its data on its owner's computer. Google access tokens are kept encrypted in a local password vault. Echo's chat history can contain answers that were drawn from email or calendar data. The owner can delete chats at any time.

Limited Use

Echo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting Gmail or Calendar in Echo's settings, or at Google Account › Third-party connections. Disconnecting deletes Echo's stored tokens.
