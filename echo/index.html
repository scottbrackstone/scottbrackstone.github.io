Echo

A private personal assistant, built and used by one person.

Echo runs on its owner's own computer. It helps with everyday planning: a daily briefing, reminders, goals, and answering questions about the owner's own calendar and email when the owner asks.

Echo is not a public service. There are no other users, no sign-ups and no accounts to create. Its connection to Google is read-only: it can look at the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar, but it cannot send, delete or change anything.

Read the privacy policy to see how Google data is handled.
